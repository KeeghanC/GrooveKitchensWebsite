## A simple way to open the meeting

> I built the demo so you could react to something real. Today I would like to confirm what the website needs to say about Groove Kitchens, decide what is included, and leave with a clear cost, timeline, and next steps. The goal is not to promise Google rankings—it is to launch a credible website that makes it easy for the right Auckland customers to find and contact you.

Keep the meeting to about 45–60 minutes. Do the walkthrough before discussing money so the scope is tangible.

## Suggested meeting flow

1. **Business goals — 5 minutes.** Ask what jobs Garth most wants, which suburbs he serves, whether he wants kitchen-only enquiries or also bathrooms, laundries, wardrobes and other cabinetry, and what makes him different.
2. **Walk through the demo — 15 minutes.** Start on a phone, then desktop. Explain that the design is responsive and the important action is to call or request a consultation.
3. **Confirm the content — 10 minutes.** Separate facts, photographs, reviews, and services that are approved for launch from placeholders.
4. **Explain the launch infrastructure — 10 minutes.** Domain, email, form delivery, hosting, analytics, Google Business Profile, and ownership.
5. **Present the scope and price — 10 minutes.** Present the Website Launch Package, then show optional extras only where Garth sees value.
6. **Close with decisions — 5 minutes.** Confirm who supplies material, dates, payment timing and the next check-in.

## Content and trust: be very clear

The current kitchen pictures are AI-generated. They are useful for the demo, but should **not** be labelled or implied to be Groove Kitchens projects. At launch, replace them with Garth’s completed-work photos wherever possible. If temporary concept images remain, label them as inspiration/concept imagery rather than project work.

The current on-site testimonials are placeholders too. Do not launch them as customer testimonials. Groove Kitchens already has strong real public proof: NoCowboys currently shows a 98.8% approval rating from 53 authenticated ratings. Ask Garth which reviews he is comfortable reusing, retain the reviewer’s wording accurately, and link to the review profile. Get approval before using names, photos, or a customer's home.

Ask Garth to provide:

Offer a separate photography quote if the available images are not strong enough. Do not bury professional photography, copywriting, paid stock licences, or extensive image editing inside the fixed website fee.

## Domain, website name, hosting and ownership

`groovekitchens.co.nz` is currently available to register. Ask Garth to register it promptly in the Groove Kitchens name; availability can change until it has been purchased. The domain registrant, registrar account, Google Business Profile, Google Analytics/Search Console, email tenant, hosting account and source-code repository should be owned by Garth/the business. You can be added as an administrator. This avoids the common problem where a business cannot move its own domain or email provider later.

If an older website or email service exists, do not change DNS blindly. First record the present DNS and email settings, identify any old indexed URLs, and plan permanent redirects from those URLs to their equivalent new pages. This protects existing email delivery and gives Google the clearest possible signal that the new site replaces the old one.

The business can continue to use Netlify's free tier initially: it includes custom domains, SSL and a CDN. Its current limits/pricing should be reviewed in Garth's own account before launch, and any paid hosting/usage cost should be his approved pass-through cost rather than an unexpected charge from you.

### Gmail transition plan

This is not a risky "conversion" of the existing `@gmail.com` account. Create a separate Google Workspace account such as `garth@groovekitchens.co.nz`; the old Gmail account stays intact and usable.

1. Verify the newly registered domain with a DNS record, create the mailbox and test it before directing any mail to it.
2. Use Google's Admin migration tool to **copy** the existing Gmail mailbox into the new account. It does not delete the original emails. Export/import only the business contacts, and keep any personal Drive files or calendar separate unless Garth wants particular items copied.
3. Put both accounts in Garth's Gmail phone app and browser. It is still the Gmail interface—he just selects the work address when replying.
4. Set the old `groovekitchens@gmail.com` account to forward new mail to the new address, while retaining copies in Gmail. Existing customers can keep using the old address; Garth sends all new quotes and replies from the new branded address.
5. After testing sending, receiving, forwarded mail and the website form, use the branded address everywhere. Keep the Gmail forwarding permanently, or at least for a long transition period.

Google's migration service requires Garth to approve access to his Gmail account. It copies mail rather than moving/deleting it, so there is a safe rollback: stop forwarding and continue using the original Gmail if anything needs attention.

## SEO: precise, honest expectations

Say this:

> I will make a genuine effort to give the website a strong technical foundation and provide Google with clear, accurate information about Groove Kitchens. I cannot promise a particular ranking, lead count, sales outcome or timeframe. Search results also depend on proximity, competitors, customer reviews, the business profile, reputation, and the quality of the information available online.

The launch work should include:

- Page titles/descriptions, sensible headings, image alt text, sitemap and robots file.
- Correct business name, phone, service areas and website link everywhere they appear.
- LocalBusiness/contractor structured data, subject to final business details.
- Google Search Console and Analytics ownership shared with Garth; submit sitemap and request indexing.
- A review of the Google Business Profile: correct primary category, service area, hours, description, real photos, website URL and review responses.
- Redirects from the old site and monitoring for indexing/technical errors after launch.

What to expect: Google says crawling can take from a few days to a few weeks after a launch or change, and requesting it does not guarantee immediate inclusion or rankings. The first useful measure is that pages are indexed and enquiries work; local visibility normally improves over time when the profile stays complete, real work/photos are added, and genuine reviews continue.

For local search, Google says results are driven mainly by relevance, distance and prominence. That makes Garth's authentic reviews, accurate service-area details, current photos and helpful replies at least as important as the website itself. Never offer discounts or rewards in exchange for Google reviews.
