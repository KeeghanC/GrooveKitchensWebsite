# Groove Kitchens Website Launch

## Meeting guide

This guide sets out what the new Groove Kitchens website will include, the options available, and the decisions needed before launch.

## The goal

Create a modern, mobile-friendly website that presents Groove Kitchens professionally, shows genuine work and customer feedback, and makes it simple for Auckland customers to call or enquire.

The website is designed to give Google clear, accurate information about the business and to support local visibility over time. It does not promise a particular Google ranking, number of enquiries, or sales result.

## What the website includes

The planned website has these areas:

- Home page with a clear introduction and enquiry action.
- Services: design and planning, cabinetry and storage, benchtops/finishes, and renovation/installation.
- Projects or inspiration gallery.
- About Groove Kitchens.
- Customer testimonials.
- Contact page with phone, email and a secure enquiry form.

The site will work across phones, tablets and desktop computers. It will include a favicon, click-to-call phone links, an email link, and a working spam-protected enquiry form.

## Domain, hosting and ownership

`groovekitchens.co.nz` is currently available to register. It should be purchased promptly in Groove Kitchens' name; domain availability can change until registration is completed.

The domain, website hosting account, Google Business Profile, Google Analytics, Search Console and email account should stay in Groove Kitchens' name. This means the business always retains control of its website, email and customer enquiries. Technical access can be shared with the website developer for setup and support.

Website hosting, HTTPS/SSL and the enquiry form can begin on Netlify's free plan. If usage ever requires a paid hosting plan, it will be discussed and approved first.

The domain registration and future renewal will be paid directly to the chosen registrar. Its price will be confirmed before purchase. If an older website host exists, it will not be cancelled until the replacement site, email and redirects have been checked.

## Professional email option

A professional address such as `garth@groovekitchens.co.nz` makes quotes, invoices and customer communication look more established than a generic Gmail address.

Recommended setup:

- One primary mailbox: `garth@groovekitchens.co.nz`.
- Aliases such as `info@`, `quotes@` and `accounts@` can all arrive in the same inbox at no extra mailbox cost.
- Google Workspace Business Starter: currently about NZ$144.90/year including GST for one mailbox on its annual plan, paid directly to Google. Provider pricing can change.
- One-off professional email setup: NZ$250, including mailbox setup, aliases, domain records, devices and testing.

The existing `groovekitchens@gmail.com` account will not be deleted or lost. Existing business email can be copied into the new inbox, and future mail sent to the old Gmail address can be forwarded to the new professional address. This lets existing customers continue using the old address while all new communication uses the branded one.

## Photos, projects and reviews

The current demo uses concept imagery to show the intended design. Before launch, it should be replaced with real Groove Kitchens project photographs wherever possible.

The website will use genuine customer reviews approved for publication. Real photos and reviews are important: they build trust and help customers see the quality of Groove Kitchens' work.

Helpful items to provide:

- 10–20 project photographs, ideally with before/after images and close details.
- A shortlist of reviews to use.
- Confirmed service list, service areas, years in business and any suppliers/credentials to mention.
- A logo file, if available, and the preferred public contact details.

## Search visibility and Google

Every reasonable effort will be made to give the website a strong technical foundation for Google: clear page content, accurate business details, page titles/descriptions, a sitemap and Search Console setup. Once launched, the sitemap will be submitted to Google and the site can be checked for indexing issues.

Google may take days or weeks to crawl a new or changed website. Local search visibility depends on many factors outside the website itself, especially relevance to the search, distance from the customer, the business's reputation, accurate details, genuine customer reviews and fresh real photos. For that reason, no specific Google ranking, number of enquiries, sales outcome or timeframe can be guaranteed.

Keeping the Google Business Profile accurate and regularly asking satisfied customers for honest reviews is one of the most valuable ongoing steps.

## Timing and revisions

The expected launch timeframe is within 2 weeks of receiving final content and registration access for the domain.

Two revision rounds are included. A revision round means one combined list of changes, rather than unlimited changes sent separately. New features, additional pages, major design changes or late content requests can be quoted before work proceeds.
