About Groove Kitchens

Groove Kitchens creates kitchens that feel considered, practical, and personal. We take the time to understand how you use your home, then turn that into a layout, materials palette, and storage plan that works beautifully every day.

From a small kitchen refresh to a full renovation, we guide you through the important decisions—from cabinetry and benchtops to appliances, lighting, and finishing details.

You can expect straightforward advice, thoughtful design, and clear communication throughout the process. Our aim is a kitchen you will enjoy using for years to come.
