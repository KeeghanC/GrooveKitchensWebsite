About Groove Kitchens

With more than 30 years of experience making and installing kitchens, Groove Kitchens creates spaces that are considered, practical, and personal.

You work directly with us from the first conversation through to installation. We take the time to visit your home or site, understand how you use the space, and turn your ideas into a kitchen that works beautifully every day.

Whether you are planning a new kitchen, a full renovation, or a thoughtful facelift, we offer straightforward advice, careful workmanship, and clear communication throughout the process. We can also create practical, well-organised laundry spaces.

Our aim is simple: to make the process enjoyable and deliver a space you will enjoy using for years to come.
